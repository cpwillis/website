Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy covers the websites and software published by cpwillis, including cpwillis.dev, gitfolio.cpwillis.dev, gitfolio.online, minigames.cpwillis.dev, skribbl.cpwillis.dev, Casefile, and the open-source repositories at github.com/cpwillis.

The short version

No personal data is deliberately collected about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no login, no payments, no newsletter, no advertising, and no third-party tracking scripts. There is no database of visitors. The sites are hosted on Cloudflare, so Cloudflare does handle ordinary request data on the author's behalf, and that is described below.

Hosting

Everything is hosted on Cloudflare. To serve and protect the sites, Cloudflare processes request data, which includes your IP address, and it may set its own security cookies in your browser. That processing is Cloudflare's, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The author does not receive a log of visitors from it and does not build any record of you from it.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics may be running on some of these sites. It is cookieless, it does not fingerprint visitors, and it reports aggregate page metrics only. No other analytics, tracking pixel, or advertising script is used.

No accounts and no collection

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay for, so no name, email address, or payment detail is ever asked for. No personal data is stored in a database, sold, or shared for marketing. There is no mailing list.

Data stored in your own browser

Some of these sites store data in your browser's local storage purely so they work, for example game progress on minigames and saved preferences elsewhere. That data stays on your device, is never transmitted to the author, and you can clear it at any time through your browser's site data settings.

Requests to third parties

Gitfolio reads GitHub's public API and loads GitHub avatars, so your browser makes requests to GitHub when you view a profile. Gitfolio also displays screenshots of the author's own deployed sites; it does not take screenshots of anyone else's. Links on these sites take you to third parties, including GitHub, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Casefile

Casefile is self-hosted software that runs locally on your own machine. Nothing it collects or produces is uploaded to the author.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. In practice there is no visitor database, so there is usually nothing held to give you. Ask via the GitHub profile below. This policy is governed by the law of Australia.

Changes

This policy may change. The current version is always the one published here, with the date at the top.

Contact

The only contact channel is the GitHub profile at github.com/cpwillis.
