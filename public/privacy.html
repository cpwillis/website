Privacy

Last updated 30 August 2026

What this covers

This policy applies to the personal projects published by cpwillis, and any personal project of mine that links here. That currently includes cpwillis.dev, gitfolio.cpwillis.dev, minigames.cpwillis.dev, skribbl.cpwillis.dev, Casefile, and the open-source repositories at github.com/cpwillis. The list is illustrative, not exhaustive: new personal projects are covered from the moment they link here.

It does not cover anything published under another name or by an organisation, including work done for clients or employers. If a site does not link to this page, this is not its privacy policy. An individual project may publish its own notice for something specific to it; where that happens, the project's own notice wins and this page is the baseline underneath it.

The short version

I do not run advertising, tracking scripts, profiling, or a mailing list, and I never sell or share anything for marketing. Most projects ask you for nothing at all. Two things are worth reading properly: the sites are hosted on Cloudflare, which necessarily handles ordinary request data, and a project with a leaderboard stores the display name you choose along with your scores.

Hosting

Everything is hosted on Cloudflare. To serve and protect the sites, Cloudflare processes request data, which includes your IP address, and it may set its own security cookies in your browser. That processing is Cloudflare's, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. I do not receive a log of visitors from it and do not build any record of you from it.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics may be running on some of these sites. It is cookieless, it does not fingerprint visitors, and it reports aggregate page metrics only. No other analytics, tracking pixel, or advertising script is used.

Projects that save a profile

Some projects keep a small saved profile so your own progress is recognised when you come back. Minigames does this. Where a project works that way, the following is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database:

  • a display name that you choose and type in
  • a random identifier generated for you, which is not derived from anything about you
  • a hashed secret held by your browser, so nobody else can claim your name
  • your results: scores, points, best times, and a history of completed attempts, with timestamps

The display name is public. It appears on the leaderboard alongside your results, visible to anyone. Choose something you are happy to publish; it does not need to be your real name and I would suggest it is not. No email address, password, or payment detail is ever asked for, and there is no sign-up form beyond picking that name.

Nothing here is used for advertising or profiling, and none of it is shared with anyone. Records are kept for as long as the leaderboard exists, since a leaderboard with the entries removed is not a leaderboard.

Data stored in your own browser

Several projects store data in your browser's local storage purely so they work, such as game progress, a saved theme, or the secret described above. That data stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time through your browser's site data settings. Clearing it means a saved profile can no longer be proven to be yours.

Requests to third parties

Gitfolio reads GitHub's public API and loads GitHub avatars, so your browser makes requests to GitHub when you view a profile. Gitfolio also displays screenshots of my own deployed sites; it does not take screenshots of anyone else's. Links on these sites take you to third parties, including GitHub, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Casefile

Casefile is self-hosted software that runs locally on your own machine. Nothing it collects or produces is uploaded to me.

Children

These sites are not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them. If a display name has been published that should not have been, ask and it will be removed.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. For most projects there is nothing held, so there is nothing to give you. Where a project saves a profile, a display name can be changed in the project itself, and a profile and its results can be deleted on request. There is currently no self-service delete button, so this is a manual request: ask via the GitHub profile below and say which display name it concerns. Data held by Cloudflare as host is governed by Cloudflare's own policy, linked above.
