Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy is published by cpwillis (the Operator). For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or complaints under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), contact the Operator at contact@cpwillis.dev (the Privacy Contact).

2. What this covers

  1. (a)This policy applies to any personal project (Project) published on GitHub by user cpwillis that links to this page, from the moment it links here.
  2. (b)For the purpose of this policy, Projects include any and all information and projects controlled by the domain name ‘cpwillis.dev’ and any websites, open-source repositories or other works published by the GitHub account cpwillis, or from any account or GitHub organisation controlled by cpwillis.
  3. (c)Where a project is governed by any policy specific to that project, or to a subsidiary, subdomain, related entity or related user (an Individual Policy), and any inconsistency arises between that Individual Policy and this document, the Individual Policy shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
  4. (d)It does not apply to anything published by an organisation which cpwillis does not control, including work done for clients or employers.

3. The short version

A Project collects only the personal information reasonably necessary for its operation. That information is used for the purpose for which it was collected and for related purposes a reasonable person would expect, or where otherwise permitted by law.

4. What may be collected

  1. (a)A Project may collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for its operation. This may include:
    1. (i)information you enter into it or give it;
    2. (ii)data generated by your use of it; and
    3. (iii)data about your device, browser and connection, including your IP address.
  2. (b)Information is collected for the purpose of operating and improving the Project and for security monitoring.
  3. (c)If you do not provide certain information, some features of a Project may not be available to you.
  4. (d)Data is stored with a third-party host provider; if applicable, the relevant third-party will be disclosed within the relevant Project.
  5. (e)A Project may also store data in your browser, such as cookies or local storage.
  6. (f)Anything you enter may be made public: assume it will be, and do not enter anything you would not want published.

5. How it may be used

  1. (a)Personal information collected by a Project will be used for the primary purpose for which it was collected.
  2. (b)It may also be used or disclosed for a secondary purpose:
    1. (i)where you have consented;
    2. (ii)where you would reasonably expect such use or disclosure having regard to the primary purpose; or
    3. (iii)where otherwise required or permitted by law.
  3. (c)Reasonable steps are taken to ensure personal information held is accurate, up-to-date, complete and relevant for the purpose for which it is held.
  4. (d)Personal information will be retained for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected and will then be destroyed or de-identified.
  5. (e)There is no backup guarantee: anything held may be reset, removed or lost at any time.

6. Hosting and third parties

Projects may be hosted by third-party hosting and infrastructure providers, and may use other third-party services. Those providers may process request data, including your IP address, keep logs, and set their own cookies in your browser, under their own privacy policies. Your browser may also make requests to third-party services directly, and data a Project displays from one may be cached.

7. Data in your browser

You can clear data stored in your browser at any time through your browser's site data settings. Doing so may mean a Project no longer recognises you.

8. Security

  1. (a)Reasonable technical and organisational steps are taken to protect personal information held from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.
  2. (b)No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Do not submit anything sensitive or confidential to any of these Projects.
  3. (c)In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm to any individual, affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner will be notified in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

9. Where data is handled

  1. (a)Data may be stored by third parties and processed anywhere in the world, including outside Australia and in countries with different privacy protections to Australia.
  2. (b)Where practicable, reasonable steps are taken to ensure that overseas recipients handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles.
  3. (c)By using a Project after reading this policy, you acknowledge and consent to your personal information being disclosed to overseas recipients in circumstances where it has not been practicable to ensure those recipients comply with the Australian Privacy Principles.
  4. (d)Relevant overseas service providers will be identified within the applicable Project where practicable.

10. Children

If you are not old enough to agree to this policy where you live, do not use these Projects.

11. Access, correction and complaints

  1. (a)Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to access personal information a Project holds about you and to request correction of any personal information that is inaccurate, out-of-date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.
  2. (b)To make an access or correction request, contact the Privacy Contact. Requests will be responded to within a reasonable time and may be refused only to the extent permitted by law.
  3. (c)If you consider that any of these Projects have handled your personal information in breach of the Australian Privacy Principles, you may lodge a complaint with the Privacy Contact.
  4. (d)If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction within 30 days, you may refer it to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.
  5. (e)This policy is governed by the law of Australia.

12. Changes

This policy may change. The current version is always the one published here, with the date at the top. Earlier versions are in the version history linked beside that date.
